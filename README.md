# My Naija Life 🇳🇬

A 3D Nigerian life-simulation game that runs in the browser on PC and mobile.

Pick your area (Ikeja, Yaba, Surulere, Lekki, Wuse 2, Bodija, Rumuola, Nassarawa GRA or New Haven), start with ₦2,000,000 and build your life: register your NIN, write JAMB, study a department, survive exams and carryovers, do SIWES, graduate, serve NYSC, pass job interviews, run side hustles, and stay out of trouble with the police.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome recommended). An internet connection is needed when the game opens, to load the 3D engine (Three.js).

| Action | PC | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / Arrow keys (Shift to run) | Drag the left side of the screen |
| Look around | Drag with the mouse, Q/R to turn, Z/X to tilt | Drag the right side of the screen |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel or ＋/－ | Pinch or ＋/－ |
| Enter / talk | E | Green button |
| Bag | I | 🎒 |
| Directory | N | 🗺️ |
| Menu | M / Esc | ☰ |

Follow the yellow dots on the ground to your next goal, and cross roads at zebra crossings.
